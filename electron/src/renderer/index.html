1. Create a Kick Developer App

This app needs API access to your Kick account.

1 Go to kick.com/settings/developer
2 Enable 2FA on your Kick account
3 Click "Create App" and give it any name
4 Set the Redirect URL to:
http://localhost:8765/callback
5 Copy your Client ID and Client Secret

2. Enter your credentials

Paste the Client ID and Client Secret from your Kick Developer App.
